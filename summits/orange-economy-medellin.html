What the Summit Was

The Orange Economy Summit in Medellín convened leaders of the creative economy, innovation, entrepreneurship and public policy to explore the economic and social value of culture and creativity.

Purpose & Positioning

The summit positioned Medellín as a regional platform for creative industries and connected young leaders with decision-makers, experts and institutions.

Program Architecture

The format combined keynote sessions, panels, youth engagement, applications, selected leaders and strong media activity.

Outcomes & Legacy

The event strengthened Medellín’s international positioning and created a visible platform for the orange economy in Latin America.
