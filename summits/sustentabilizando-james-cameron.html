What the Summit Was

Sustentabilizando with James Cameron brought one of the world’s most influential cultural figures into a high-visibility sustainability conversation in Buenos Aires.

Purpose & Positioning

The event used the power of storytelling, culture and public leadership to broaden the conversation around climate change, sustainability and responsible transformation.

Program Architecture

The format combined a major public conversation, keynote-style interventions, moderated dialogue and media visibility, supported by additional speakers and institutional partners.

Outcomes & Legacy

The event generated strong public and media attention, reinforcing ALF’s ability to convene global figures around sustainability and transform them into platforms of awareness and action.
