What the Summit Was

The 1st Circular Economy & Innovation Summit in Madrid was one of ALF’s landmark platforms, gathering global leaders, Nobel Laureates, former heads of state, innovators and institutions around sustainability and circular innovation.

Purpose & Positioning

The summit positioned Madrid as a global meeting point for circular economy, technological innovation and sustainable competitiveness, linking public policy, business leadership and social awareness.

Program Architecture

The program included keynote speeches, high-level conversations, panels, public sessions and extensive media visibility. The participation of President Barack Obama gave the event exceptional international profile.

Outcomes & Legacy

The summit set a new benchmark for ALF’s convening capacity and helped establish circular economy as a major institutional and public conversation across Europe and Latin America.
