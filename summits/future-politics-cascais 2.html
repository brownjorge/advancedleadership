What the Summit Was

The Future of Politics Summit in Cascais created a high-level space to examine democratic leadership, political renewal and the future of governance after a period of global disruption.

Purpose & Positioning

The summit connected former presidents, ministers, academics, public thinkers and emerging leaders to discuss the changing expectations of citizens and the institutions that must respond to them.

Program Architecture

The platform combined plenaries, panel discussions, keynote sessions, interviews, networking and institutional participation, giving Cascais a visible role in the global conversation on political leadership.

Outcomes & Legacy

The summit reinforced ALF’s ability to convene political and intellectual leaders across ideologies and geographies around the future of democratic life.
