What the Summit Was

The 2nd Green Economy Summit in Córdoba deepened ALF’s sustainability platform in Argentina, bringing international leaders and experts into dialogue with local institutions and communities.

Purpose & Positioning

The summit reinforced Córdoba’s position as a regional sustainability reference and expanded public engagement around green economy, innovation and responsible development.

Program Architecture

The program included high-level speeches, expert panels, leadership activities, media engagement and institutional participation across multiple sustainability themes.

Outcomes & Legacy

The summit consolidated ALF’s green economy platform and strengthened the link between international debate and local capacity-building in Latin America.
