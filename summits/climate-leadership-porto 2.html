What the Summit Was

The Climate Change Leadership Summit in Porto addressed the impact of climate change on agriculture, wine and society, convening experts and leaders around practical pathways for adaptation and mitigation.

Purpose & Positioning

The summit connected climate science, industry leadership and public awareness, showing how specific sectors can drive broader climate responsibility.

Program Architecture

The program combined keynote presentations, scientific contributions, industry discussions and public-facing leadership conversations.

Outcomes & Legacy

The summit contributed to an international conversation on climate leadership and helped translate sector-specific vulnerability into broader public action.
