What the Summit Was

The Future of Social Networks with Randi Zuckerberg convened a major public conversation in Buenos Aires around the future of technology, digital platforms and communication.

Purpose & Positioning

The event positioned Buenos Aires as a stage for forward-looking discussion on social media, innovation, entrepreneurship and the cultural impact of digital transformation.

Program Architecture

The platform included a public conversation, recognition moments, sponsor visibility, media coverage and institutional participation.

Outcomes & Legacy

The event reached a broad audience and demonstrated ALF’s capacity to design high-impact public conferences with globally recognized innovators and thought leaders.
