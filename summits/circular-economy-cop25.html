What the Summit Was

The 2nd Circular Economy Summit at COP25 connected circular economy and climate policy within the broader United Nations climate agenda hosted in Madrid.

Purpose & Positioning

The summit advanced the conversation on how circular economy principles can support climate ambition, competitiveness and sustainable development across sectors.

Program Architecture

The program brought public and private leaders together through panels, institutional dialogues and expert-led sessions within the broader COP25 climate context.

Outcomes & Legacy

The summit helped connect circular economy thinking to global climate policy and reinforced ALF’s presence in international climate conversations.
