What the Summit Was

The 1st Green Economy Summit: Consciousness and Action marked the beginning of ALF’s large-scale sustainability platforms in Córdoba, Argentina.

Purpose & Positioning

The summit was designed to create public awareness, mobilize young leaders and connect international expertise with local sustainability priorities.

Program Architecture

The program combined major speeches, training components, public participation and international visibility around green economy and conscious leadership.

Outcomes & Legacy

The summit established the foundation for ALF’s later sustainability work and helped transform Córdoba into a platform for environmental leadership in Latin America.
