Leadership training as a cornerstone

Practical, international and transformative executive education.

Since its founding, ALF has emphasized that leadership is not only about vision, but also about preparation. Leaders need structured, high-level programs that equip them with tools to address the challenges of today and anticipate the opportunities of tomorrow.

ALF’s Executive Courses and Certificates blend rigorous academic content with applied learning, mentoring and real-world case studies. The portfolio covers cybersecurity, women’s empowerment, sustainable business models, international trade and the green economy.

Each program reflects ALF’s philosophy that executive training must leave participants not only with knowledge, but with skills, networks and a sense of responsibility to act.

Green Business Accelerator

Turning sustainability into competitiveness.

The Green Business Accelerator Certificate is one of ALF’s flagship executive training programs. Developed in alliance with the Green Banking Academy of the International Finance Corporation (IFC), part of the World Bank Group, the program helps business leaders, entrepreneurs, public officials and private-sector representatives understand sustainability as a competitive advantage.

The certificate fills a practical niche: rather than treating climate change only as a technical or ethical issue, it equips participants with tools to identify market opportunities, assess risks, mobilize internal transformation and strengthen the resilience and profitability of their organizations.

Delivered as an accelerated, hands-on format, the program gives participants the skills and frameworks needed to transform sustainability from an obligation into a source of innovation, financing opportunities, cost efficiency and long-term value.

Certificate focus

Four practical learning axes

The program is designed to help organizations understand the business case for sustainability and apply practical tools in real operating environments.

Sustainability as a business opportunity affecting competitiveness and profitability.Identification and analysis of the risks of not adopting sustainability principles.Identification of the specific opportunities that climate change creates for each organization.Practical tools to implement sustainability measures and mobilize internal transformation.
Regional implementations

Global standards. Local application.

The Green Business Accelerator Certificate has been implemented in Galicia, Seville, the Basque Country, the Canary Islands, Valencia and Valladolid, adapting global sustainability standards to regional economic realities.

Looking forward, ALF is preparing to expand this model to Argentina and Gibraltar, strengthening its international scope and positioning it as a benchmark in sustainability training.
