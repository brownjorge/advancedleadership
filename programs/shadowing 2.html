Leadership Shadowing Programs

Experiencing leadership first-hand by observing how senior executives, policymakers and innovators make decisions, manage teams and confront complex challenges.

Experiencing leadership first-hand

Leadership is not only taught. It is demonstrated.

Building on the philosophy that leadership is best learned through direct exposure, ALF created the Shadowing Program to allow emerging leaders to step inside the daily work of top executives, policymakers and innovators.

Unlike traditional internships, the Shadowing Program offers a close-up perspective of leadership itself. Participants observe how senior figures make decisions, manage teams, address crises and balance competing priorities.

The experience shapes not only their careers but also their understanding of how leaders can drive positive change in a complex and interconnected world.

Washington, D.C.

Strategic bridges

Global reach

Multiple nationalities

Participants from Gibraltar, Israel, Ukraine, the United Kingdom and Spain have taken part in shadowing experiences, enriching the program with diverse perspectives and backgrounds.

Public and private leadership

From boardrooms to government

Participants observe strategic decision-making in business and public policy environments, from day-to-day operations to high-level institutional conversations.
