Summits

ALF summits are high-level platforms where global leaders, Nobel Laureates, former heads of state, CEOs, academics and local communities meet to transform urgent challenges into visibility, alliances and action.

What we have built

Platforms that made cities part of the global conversation.

From Córdoba and Medellín to Madrid, Barcelona, Vicuña, Fortaleza and beyond, ALF has helped transform host cities into global stages for sustainability, circular economy, energy transition, innovation, tourism and democratic leadership. Each summit was designed not merely as a conference, but as a catalyst for media visibility, international partnerships and lasting institutional credibility.

A record of global convening

A record of global convening, local empowerment and measurable impact.

Across more than a decade, ALF has convened presidents, ministers, Nobel Laureates, CEOs, scientists, entrepreneurs, students and civil society leaders in platforms designed to move beyond dialogue. Each summit combined international visibility with local empowerment, helping host cities and regions become reference points for the global conversations shaping sustainability, innovation, energy, tourism and democratic leadership.

The following record presents ALF’s principal summits and high-level platforms. Each card opens into a dedicated summit profile with context, positioning, program architecture, outcomes and a curated visual gallery.
