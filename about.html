A message from the President

From an ambitious vision to a global platform.

When the Advanced Leadership Foundation began its journey, it was built on a simple conviction: leadership can be cultivated, strengthened and multiplied when people are given access to the right knowledge, networks and opportunities.

Over more than a decade, that conviction has become a global body of work. ALF has helped prepare and connect the next generation of leaders, convened world leaders, Nobel Laureates, entrepreneurs, academics and young professionals, and forged partnerships with governments, multilateral organizations and private institutions across continents.

Our work has unfolded through executive leadership training programs, certified and multilateral trade missions, and international summits that have placed cities, provinces and institutions at the center of global conversations on sustainability, innovation, energy transition, tourism, economic development and social progress.

The years ahead will demand bold leadership, collaborative thinking and a renewed commitment to sustainable and inclusive growth. ALF will continue to expand its training programs, strengthen its international alliances and create platforms where ideas become commitments and leadership becomes impact.

Juan Verde
President

Who we are

A Foundation Built on Purpose and Partnership

The Advanced Leadership Foundation (ALF) was established in Washington, D.C. in 2014 as a non-profit organization with one central mission: to equip and empower the next generation of global leaders. Over the past decade, ALF has expanded its footprint with operating offices in Madrid, Spain, and Buenos Aires, Argentina, and has built an international reputation as a platform for leadership, innovation and cooperation.

Our purpose is simple yet ambitious: to close the gap between talent and opportunity. Across the globe, companies, governments and institutions often struggle to find leaders with the right skills to meet today’s complex challenges. At the same time, young professionals, entrepreneurs and public servants seek meaningful opportunities to learn, to serve and to grow. ALF bridges this gap by creating programs that connect people, institutions and markets — programs that transform potential into action and ideas into impact.

Through executive leadership training, certified and multilateral trade missions, and landmark international summits, ALF has built a global ecosystem of leaders committed to a more sustainable, inclusive and innovative world.

Mission

Empower global leaders

To equip and empower the next generation of global leaders with the knowledge, resources, skills and networks required to generate meaningful change in their communities, institutions and industries.

Vision

A more connected world

To foster a more competitive, sustainable and interconnected world by building bridges among governments, companies, academia, civil society and emerging leaders.

Purpose

Turn potential into impact

To create platforms where leadership development strengthens economies, opens opportunities, connects cultures and produces long-term value for people, institutions and communities.

Our guiding principles

Leadership becomes impact when people, institutions and markets are connected.

Leadership multiplies impact

One trained leader influences entire organizations, communities and sectors — creating ripple effects that extend far beyond individual growth.

Partnerships are essential

Real progress occurs when governments, companies, academia and civil society work together toward shared goals.

Global challenges require global solutions

Leadership must transcend borders, uniting talent from different countries and cultures to respond to sustainability, innovation and social inclusion.

Bridging the gap through our points of connection

Emerging leaders

Young professionals gain access to international experiences, executive training and networks that help them move from academic preparation to positions of responsibility.

Governments and institutions

Public institutions strengthen their capacity, modernize strategies and train promising leaders through programs, summits and trade missions.

Companies and the private sector

Companies develop new talent and expand competitiveness through international partnerships, sustainable investments and global exposure.

Through these points of connection, ALF builds bridges that not only empower individuals but also strengthen economies and communities, ensuring that leadership development has a multiplying effect across society.
