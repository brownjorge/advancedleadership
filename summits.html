Summits

ALF summits are high-level platforms where global leaders, Nobel Laureates, former heads of state, CEOs, academics and local communities meet to transform urgent challenges into visibility, alliances and action.

What we have built

Platforms that made cities part of the global conversation.

From Córdoba and Medellín to Madrid, Barcelona, Vicuña, Fortaleza and beyond, ALF has helped transform host cities into global stages for sustainability, circular economy, energy transition, innovation, tourism and democratic leadership. Each summit was designed not merely as a conference, but as a catalyst for media visibility, international partnerships and lasting institutional credibility.

Summits as platforms for transformation

Our experience turns global summits into engines of leadership, visibility and lasting impact.

For more than a decade, ALF has designed and delivered international summits that do far more than gather audiences. Our platforms connect global leaders with local communities, position cities and regions on the world stage, create institutional credibility, accelerate partnerships, and generate economic, social and reputational impact.

Each summit is built as a strategic intervention: a place where ideas become alliances, leadership is multiplied, and territories gain visibility around the issues that define their future.
